How to read this chart

2025 colours are test scores. The original GDPval study tested AI on 5 tasks for each job. We show the best score of 7 AI models. A score shows how often AI did as well as, or better than, a human expert.

2026 colours are estimates. There are no newer GDPval scores for each job. We used GPT-5.5's published overall score of 84.9% and Anthropic's 2026 task data to estimate scores by job. These estimates were not tested on the original tasks.

2026 tasks come from real AI use. We linked tasks from Claude chats to jobs using O*NET codes. AI, not human experts, rated how often Claude succeeded. Seeing a task in the data does not mean AI can do the whole job.

The slider moves smoothly between the two views. It does not show results measured between 2025 and 2026.
